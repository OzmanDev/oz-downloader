# Orb background

## 2026-10-10 — Three dim circles behind every Mac screen

Mac only. One layer behind Get Music, My Playlists, Preferences, and Help, including the gaps around the cards. The cards stay solid. The What’s new sheet stays solid. Windows is unchanged. The circles do not take clicks.

Three circles drift slowly: the app’s blue, violet, and teal. They stay dim so the type stays readable. On a 1000 by 700 window, each circle moves less than 30 points in one second. They are not stacked on the same spot. The same time always puts them in the same place.
