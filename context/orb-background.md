# Orb background

## 2026-10-10 — Three dim circles behind every Mac screen

Mac only. One layer behind Get Music, My Playlists, Preferences, and Help. Cards, buttons, and lists use the system glass, so the glow shows through them. Windows is unchanged. The circles do not take clicks.

Three circles drift so the motion is easy to see: the app’s blue, violet, and teal. They stay dim so the type stays readable. On a 1000 by 700 window, each circle travels more than 240 points in 4 seconds or less, faster than 60 points a second. The blur is painted once and the circle slides, so the glow actually moves. The areas around the cards are clear, so the glow shows through. Reduce Motion holds them at the start. They are not stacked on the same spot.

Each pass picks a new arrangement. The three colors start on three different edges — leading, trailing, top, or bottom — and any color can take any of those edges. They travel to one shared meeting point, then leave toward a different set of edges. The next meeting is not the reverse of the path they just took. A pass is chosen once and kept until that leg finishes, so the circles do not jump on every frame.
