# Orb background

## 2026-10-10 — Three dim circles behind every Mac screen

Mac only. One layer behind Get Music, My Playlists, Preferences, and Help, including the gaps around the cards. The cards stay solid. The What’s new sheet stays solid. Windows is unchanged. The circles do not take clicks.

Three circles drift slowly: the app’s blue, violet, and teal. They stay dim so the type stays readable. On a 1000 by 700 window, each circle travels more than 120 points from its start to its end, and that trip takes long enough that the speed stays under 30 points a second. The blur is painted once and the circle slides, so the glow actually moves. The areas around the cards are clear, so the glow shows through. Reduce Motion holds them at the start. They are not stacked on the same spot.
